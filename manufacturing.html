FAQ

Security, compliance and practical concerns

Straight answers to the questions buyers ask before a first conversation.

Does Business Central support manufacturing?
Business Central includes manufacturing capabilities such as production BOMs, routings and production orders. We'll confirm fit against your processes.
Can you migrate from Dynamics GP?
Yes, GP to Business Central is a common path for manufacturers.
How do you avoid disrupting production?
Cutover is planned around production schedules with testing and fallback plans.
How do you handle costing and traceability?
We agree your costing method early, whether standard, average or actual, and test it before go-live. Lot and serial tracking is configured to your own traceability obligations.
Can shop-floor data feed our reporting?
Yes. Production reporting is designed for the people on the floor to use, and that data can then be reviewed alongside inventory and finance in Power BI, so cost and performance are visible in one place.
