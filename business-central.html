Supply chain

  • Purchasing and vendor management

  • Planning and demand forecasting

03. Project management

Project management

See what every project costs and earns while it is running, not after it has closed.

  • Project and job costing

  • Timesheets

  • Billing against planned or actual cost

  • Profitability reporting by project

04. Sales and customers

Sales and customers

Take an order from quote to invoice in the same system that holds the customer, the stock and the ledger.

  • Invoicing and subscription billing

  • Relationship and segment management

Reporting and analytics

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers to key questions about Business Central, helping you evaluate the platform and plan the path forward.

Why Business Central?
A modern cloud ERP that connects finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, projects, reporting, and operations on one platform, with the broader Microsoft ecosystem.
How will we migrate our data and processes?
A structured migration approach assesses existing processes and data, cleanses and maps required information, migrates it to Business Central, and validates it through testing before go-live.
How does Business Central integrate with our existing ecosystem?
Business Central connects with Microsoft 365, Teams, Excel, Power BI, Power Platform, Copilot, Azure, and third-party applications to create connected business processes and reduce disconnected systems.
How secure and scalable is the platform?
Business Central is a Microsoft cloud solution with role-based access, identity and security controls, audit capabilities, and cloud infrastructure designed to support organizations as their usage and operations grow.
How quickly can we implement and realize value?
Implementation follows a structured path from discovery and design through configuration, migration, testing, training, go-live, and optimization, allowing value to be delivered progressively based on scope and complexity.
How is Business Central licensed?
Business Central uses a flexible subscription model with Essentials, Premium, and Team Member licenses, allowing organizations to align access and capabilities with each user’s role.
