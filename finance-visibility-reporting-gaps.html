Objection Handling

Questions we hear most

Answering objections before the demo removes friction that silently kills conversion.

We migrated systems recently — is this even our problem?
Usually the gap isn't the platform, it's disconnected configuration and manual workarounds layered on top after go-live. A quick diagnostic shows whether the issue is the system itself or how it's wired together.
How disruptive is this during month-end close?
We deliberately phase work around your close calendar — discovery and design happen in parallel, and cut-over is scheduled for a low-risk window. Most clients see no disruption to an active close.
We already have Power BI — why isn't this solved?
Power BI visualises whatever it's fed. If the underlying data is fragmented or ungoverned, the dashboards inherit that mess. We fix the source of truth first, so BI finally becomes trustworthy.
How long before we see measurable value?
Most finance engagements reach a first measurable milestone (a faster close or a governed reporting layer) within the first few weeks — not quarters. We scope for early, provable wins.
Do we need to replace everything at once?
No. We route to the smallest change that resolves the root cause — often integration or a governed data layer before any core replacement. You modernise in the order that de-risks the business.
