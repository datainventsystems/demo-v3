Objection Handling

Questions we hear most

Answering objections before the demo removes friction that silently kills conversion.

Do we need to replace our finance system?
Not necessarily. Discovery should determine whether the main constraint is process, configuration, integration, reporting or the core platform. The recommendation may be to improve the current environment, add an analytics layer or plan a phased ERP move.
Can dashboards fix the problem first?
Dashboards can improve access, but they will not make inconsistent source data or an uncontrolled close reliable. Reporting work should be paired with data ownership, definitions and the process that produces the numbers.
Will automation remove finance controls?
Automation should reinforce approved controls by routing work, recording decisions and highlighting exceptions. Control design and approval authority must be agreed before automation is configured.
How much historical data should move?
That depends on statutory, audit, reporting and operational needs. The team should classify data that must be migrated, retained for reference or archived before estimating effort.
How long will this take?
Timing depends on scope, data condition, integrations, entities and change readiness. A focused discovery produces a phased plan rather than an unsupported standard timeline.
