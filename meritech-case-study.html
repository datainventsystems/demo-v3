The customer

Engineering built on integrity, quality and client focus.

Meritech Engineering is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of integrity and quality, with a clear focus on client needs. The company excels in municipal design and construction, offering innovative, cost-effective, and environmentally sustainable solutions.

Backed by a collaborative team with a diverse skill set and extensive experience, Meritech Engineering is well-prepared to meet client demands. As the industry progresses, the firm takes a proactive approach to project ownership and results delivery, emphasizing not just infrastructure development but also the enhancement of community well-being.

The challenge

An outdated system that could not keep up with modern demands.

01

Outdated for modern demands

The outdated system struggled with modern demands, leading to consideration of an upgrade or replacement. Its limited configuration capabilities restricted customization.

02

Complex and unintuitive

The user interface was complex and unintuitive, reducing operational efficiency.

03

Weak reporting and data silos

Inadequate reporting features failed to provide useful data analysis, affecting decision-making, and the lack of integration with financial systems caused data silos.

Where the old system fell short
Limited configurationComplex user interfaceInadequate reportingNo financial system integration
The solution

Dynamics 365 Project Service Automation, connected to finance.

DataInvent Systems recommended Dynamics 365 Project Service Automation for its robust capabilities in sales process management, project management, and employee management. The CRM’s powerful editing features allowed for the customization of tables to enhance the client’s daily data management.

SSRS was used to create reports that support the management team in making informed decisions. The system’s APIs facilitated the seamless transfer of sales contracts to the financial backend, and Power Automate was employed for custom integrations with SharePoint and QuickBooks, as well as for various email notifications.
