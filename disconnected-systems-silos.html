Objection Handling

Questions technology and operations leaders ask

Practical answers before you decide whether integration, data architecture or platform modernization is worth exploring.

Do we have to replace everything?
No. Integration and data design should preserve systems that still serve a clear purpose. Replacement is appropriate only when a platform cannot support required control, scale, usability or connectivity.
Should we integrate systems or move to one platform?
The answer depends on process ownership, data volume, latency, security, total cost and long-term architecture. Discovery should compare both options against the same business outcomes.
Can reporting become the single source of truth?
A reporting model can create consistent definitions and views, but operational systems still need clear ownership and reliable data flows. Analytics does not replace transaction governance.
How do we avoid another fragile integration?
Define ownership, error handling, monitoring, security, support and change control before build. An integration is an ongoing product, not a one-time connection.
Where do we begin?
Start with one high-value business event such as lead-to-order, order-to-cash or client-request-to-billing and map the systems and data involved.
