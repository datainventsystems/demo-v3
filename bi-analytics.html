FAQ

BI & Analytics questions

Straight answers to common questions before a first conversation.

Do we need Microsoft Fabric before using Power BI?
Not always. The architecture should follow the data sources, scale, governance and analytics requirements rather than forcing every organization into the same platform sequence.
Can you connect data from non-Microsoft systems?
Yes. Discovery identifies the source systems, integration constraints, ownership and refresh requirements needed for the target analytics experience.
How do you prevent different teams from reporting different numbers?
The solution establishes governed definitions, ownership, semantic models and access patterns so shared measures are calculated consistently.
Will business users be able to explore data themselves?
Self-service can be enabled within governed models and permissions, giving users flexibility without recreating uncontrolled reporting silos.
What happens to our existing reports?
Existing reports are assessed for audience, value, duplication, data source and migration priority. Useful content can be retained or redesigned within the target architecture.
