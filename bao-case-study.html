The customer

A government-delegated regulator for Ontario’s bereavement sector.

The Bereavement Authority of Ontario (BAO) operates as a government-delegated body and a non-profit organization, overseeing the implementation of the Funeral, Burial, and Cremation Services Act, 2002 (FBCSA).

BAO is tasked with regulation and ensuring adherence to legal standards, while also providing support and services to the licensed operators and professionals it serves throughout Ontario.

Who BAO serves
  • Funeral establishment operators
  • Funeral directors
  • Preplanners
  • Cemetery operators
  • Crematorium operators
  • Alternative disposition operators
  • Transfer service operators
  • Sales representatives
The challenge

An outdated system, and an audit that exposed the gaps.

2016

An outdated system from the start

Since its 2016 inception, BAO had used an outdated system lacking adequate regulatory features for licensing, inspections, complaints, and information management.

2020

The audit highlights significant flaws

The 2020 audit by the Office of the Auditor General of Ontario highlighted significant flaws, particularly in protecting consumers during sensitive times.

RFP

BAO launches a Request for Proposal

In response, BAO launched a Request for Proposal (RFP) to update its technology and business infrastructure.

Where the old system fell short
LicensingInspectionsComplaintsInformation management
The solution

One regulatory platform, built on Microsoft.

The project revamped BAO’s systems by implementing Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service and integrating DataInvent Systems’ regulatory tools, Civic Cloud.

A new licensee portal, built with Microsoft Power Pages, improved digital services. The updated infrastructure features SharePoint Online, Power Automate and Power BI, and integrates PayPal and QuickBooks, enhancing operational efficiency.
