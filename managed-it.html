FAQ

Managed IT questions

Straight answers to common questions before a first conversation.

What is included in Managed IT?
Only the systems, services, hours and responsibilities listed in the approved service catalogue and agreement. The public page should avoid implying universal coverage.
Do you support business applications?
Yes, where included. DataInvent has material for Dynamics 365 Sales application support and enhancement; other application services must be confirmed.
Are response and resolution times guaranteed?
Use only commitments approved by service operations and documented in the applicable agreement. Do not publish generic targets from one customer contract.
Can you take over from another provider?
Potentially, after access, documentation, dependencies, risks and transition responsibilities are assessed.
Can support include improvements?
Yes, where enhancement planning and delivery are included or separately approved. Operational support and project work should remain clearly distinguished.
