FAQ

Managed IT questions

Straight answers to common questions before a first conversation.

Does Managed IT replace our internal IT team?
It can complement an internal team by taking defined operational responsibilities, specialist workloads or support coverage while preserving clear ownership and escalation.
Which Microsoft environments can be included?
The service scope can be designed around Microsoft 365, Azure, Dynamics 365, Power Platform and connected systems based on the assessment and agreed responsibility model.
How is security included in the service?
Security responsibilities can include identity, access, policy, monitoring, posture improvement and escalation, with exact coverage defined in the service design.
Can Managed IT help with legacy systems and modernization?
Yes. Operational support can be paired with lifecycle and modernization planning so recurring risk and technical debt are addressed rather than only maintained.
How will we know whether service is improving?
The operating model should define service reporting, priorities, trends, recurring issues, risk and improvement actions so performance is visible over time.
