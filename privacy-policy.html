07Meeting Recording and Transcription

DataInvent Systems Corp uses Microsoft Teams to conduct client, partner, and internal meetings. Where meeting recording and transcription features are enabled, the following guidelines apply:

  • When Recording Occurs: Meetings may be recorded when required for documentation, support, project delivery, training, or quality assurance purposes. The meeting host is responsible for notifying all participants prior to or at the start of the recording.
  • What Is Captured: Recorded meetings may include audio, video, screen sharing, chat messages, and AI‑generated transcripts produced through Microsoft Teams.
  • How We Use This Information: Recorded and transcribed content is used solely for legitimate business purposes, including meeting documentation, project continuity, internal review, and team training. Recordings are not sold and are not shared with third parties outside of authorised DataInvent personnel and, where applicable, the client or partner organisation involved in the meeting.
  • Retention and Storage: Meeting recordings and transcripts are stored securely in accordance with DataInvent Systems Corp’s internal data retention policies and applicable privacy legislation, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) in Canada and applicable United States state privacy laws.
  • Your Rights: Participants may request information about how recorded or transcribed data is handled or request deletion where applicable under governing privacy laws. To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@datainvent.com.
