FAQ

AI & Copilot questions

Straight answers to common questions before a first conversation.

Do we need an enterprise-wide AI strategy before starting?
No. A focused roadmap can begin with a small set of valuable and feasible use cases while establishing the governance and information foundations needed to learn responsibly.
How do you choose the right Copilot or AI use cases?
Use cases are compared using business value, feasibility, information readiness, risk, ownership and adoption effort.
How is governance included without slowing everything down?
Governance is designed into discovery and delivery, clarifying acceptable use, access, human review, accountability and escalation early.
Can AI work with our existing Microsoft environment?
The target experience can use Microsoft 365, Power Platform, Azure, Fabric and business applications, with access and integration defined for the specific scenario.
How do you measure whether AI adoption is working?
Measures should reflect the intended workflow, such as quality, cycle time, usage, exceptions and user adoption, rather than tool access alone.
