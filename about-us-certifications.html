Message from the Chairman

Turning complexity into decisive clarity.

At DataInvent, we believe technology creates its greatest value when it is grounded in a clear understanding of the business, the people who operate it, and the outcomes leadership is working to achieve.

Our role is not simply to implement platforms. We work alongside clients to simplify complexity, connect disconnected processes and information, and build practical foundations for better decisions and sustainable growth. That means listening carefully, challenging assumptions when necessary, and keeping every technology choice tied to a real operational or strategic priority.

As the Microsoft ecosystem continues to evolve across cloud, business applications, data, automation, and AI, DataInvent remains focused on helping organizations adopt those capabilities with confidence. We are committed to thoughtful delivery, accountable partnerships, and solutions that continue to serve the business well beyond go-live.

Our Journey

Progress shaped by purpose.

From a Canadian consulting practice to a growing global Microsoft technology partner, each chapter has expanded DataInvent's ability to help organizations transform.

201220162018202020242026
2012

The Beginning

DataInvent is founded.

DataInvent was founded and opened its first office in Mississauga, Ontario, launching as a technology consulting and business solutions company focused on helping organizations accelerate digital transformation.

2016

Microsoft Business Applications Partnership

Microsoft capability grows.

DataInvent achieved Microsoft Business Applications Partner status, strengthening its expertise in delivering innovative Dynamics 365 and Power Platform solutions to customers.

2018

Nationwide Expansion

Serving clients across Canada.

Building on its early success, DataInvent expanded its reach across Canada, serving clients from coast to coast and growing its team of technology specialists.

2020

Microsoft Solutions Excellence

All six designations achieved.

DataInvent earned all six Microsoft Solution Partner Designations, demonstrating broad capabilities and deep expertise across Microsoft's cloud and business technology ecosystem.

2024

Expansion into the United States

Growing across North America.

DataInvent entered the U.S. market, bringing its proven consulting, cloud, and business application services to organizations across North America.

2026

Global Growth

A broader international presence.

DataInvent expanded internationally with new operations in the UAE, Morocco, and Asia, marking a major milestone in its journey toward becoming a truly global technology partner.
