FAQ

Modern Work questions

Straight answers to common questions before a first conversation.

Do we need to replace all our current collaboration tools?
Not necessarily. Discovery identifies which tools and work patterns should be retained, integrated, consolidated or retired based on business and governance needs.
How do you improve Teams and SharePoint governance?
Governance can define ownership, naming, access, sharing, lifecycle, retention and support responsibilities while keeping the user experience practical.
Can Modern Work include workflow automation?
Yes. Power Platform can support approvals, requests, notifications and lightweight apps connected to Microsoft 365 work.
How do you support user adoption?
Adoption planning includes stakeholder involvement, role-based communications, training, champions and measurement tied to the targeted work patterns.
Can Copilot be part of the Modern Work roadmap?
Yes. Copilot readiness depends on information access, content governance, security and the use cases that matter in everyday work.
