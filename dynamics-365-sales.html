FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Practical answers for teams evaluating Dynamics 365 Sales and planning the next step.

What is Dynamics 365 Sales?
Dynamics 365 Sales is Microsoft's sales application. It brings leads, accounts, opportunities and forecasting into one system, and it works with Outlook, Teams and the rest of Microsoft 365.
Do we need to replace our current CRM all at once?
Not necessarily. Discovery identifies the priority data, integrations and teams, so the move can be phased around business value and continuity.
How does it work with Microsoft 365?
Sellers can work with customer records from Outlook and Teams, keep email and meetings connected to the right record, and open data in Excel. That reduces switching between applications and manual re-entry.
How do you improve adoption?
Adoption starts with a design that fits how your sellers work: fewer required fields, practical role-based training, and clear ownership of data and process. Reporting after launch shows where more help is needed.
