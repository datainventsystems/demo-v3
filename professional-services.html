FAQ

Utilization, delivery and practical concerns

Straight answers to common questions professional-services buyers consider when improving utilization, project visibility, profitability and workflow execution.

Why focus on utilization and profitability together?
The approved Professional Services positioning emphasizes both resource utilization and profitability, so the industry journey connects people and delivery decisions with financial visibility.
Can modernization connect finance, collaboration and workflow systems?
Integration and compatibility are explicit requirements for technical evaluators in the approved buyer journey, so the existing stack should be part of the modernization assessment.
Where does AI fit for professional-services teams?
AI and automation should be connected to clear business problems, workflow relevance and governance rather than treated as a standalone technology objective.
