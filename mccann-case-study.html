The customer

A family-owned, one-stop shop for concrete construction.

McCann Redi-Mix Inc., a family-owned business established in 1953, operates out of Mt. Carmel, Ontario, with additional plants in Exeter, Mitchell, and Watford.

The company specializes in redi-mix concrete and provides a range of services including concrete construction, excavation, aggregates, and drag-lining. Its distinctive “one-stop shop” approach ensures high-quality, efficient, and timely execution of projects, and it manages all aspects of projects, from design and budgeting to scheduling, material supply, and construction.

What McCann offers
  • Redi-mix concrete
  • Concrete construction

The challenge

Paper-based methods and systems that did not connect.

01

Paper-based data management

The company relied on traditional paper-based methods for managing and storing dynamic data, which required significant manpower and time for monthly verification.

02

Hard to preserve and retrieve

Data preservation posed challenges, and retrieving specific information was often cumbersome.

03

Manual entry, disconnected systems

Manual document entry by drivers and workers led to data inconsistencies, and the multiple software systems used for sales and daily operations did not integrate well across platforms.

Where the old way fell short
Manpower and timeData preservationData retrievalData inconsistenciesSystems that did not integrate
The solution

A customized Dynamics 365 solution, with a mobile app.

DataInvent Systems implemented a Microsoft Dynamics 365 solution, customizing it to address the client’s specific issues and needs. This transition eliminated paper-based methods, with all data now stored in the cloud.

A mobile app was developed for the client, enabling drivers to complete work orders, clock in, access project details, browse tasks, and perform various operations from their mobile devices anytime, anywhere. By utilizing Dynamics CRM’s powerful API, data across different platforms now integrates seamlessly.
