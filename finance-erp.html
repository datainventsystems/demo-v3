FAQ

Finance & ERP questions

Straight answers to common questions before a first conversation.

Is Business Central right for every organisation?
No. Fit depends on process complexity, entities, countries, volume, industry requirements, integrations and internal capacity. Discovery should validate the platform before scope is committed.
Can we start with finance only?
Often, yes. A finance-first phase can create value while preserving a roadmap for inventory, projects, service or other functions, provided dependencies and integrations are addressed.
Can you automate accounts payable?
Yes, where invoice sources, validation, approval rules and exception handling are defined. The exact capability depends on the selected platform and approved scope.
Will historical data be migrated?
Only the history required for operations, reporting, compliance and audit should be migrated. Other data may be retained in a governed archive.
How is project risk controlled?
Use agreed scope, fit-gap decisions, data reconciliation, role-based testing, acceptance criteria, change control, cutover planning and executive ownership.
