MONTH-END CLOSE

From manual chase to controlled close.

For finance teams, the pain is not only the close date. The real issue is the uncontrolled work behind it: chasing owners, reconciling spreadsheets, checking versions and explaining variances late.

BEFORE

Teams chase inputs across email, spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

WHAT CHANGES

Finance workflows, approvals and reconciliations are standardised inside the Microsoft ecosystem.

AFTER

Close activity becomes visible, repeatable and easier to manage across teams.
