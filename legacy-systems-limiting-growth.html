Objection Handling

Questions leaders ask before modernizing

Practical answers before deciding what to retain, replace, re-platform or modernize in stages.

Can we modernize without a big-bang replacement?
Yes. A phased approach can separate discovery, data preparation, core process transition, integrations and later enhancements. The sequence should protect critical operating and reporting cycles.
What should move first?
Start with the process that creates the strongest combination of risk, business value and readiness. Dependencies and data quality matter as much as business urgency.
How much customization should we keep?
Keep what supports a real differentiator or mandatory requirement. Challenge customizations that only recreate an old way of working when standard capability can meet the outcome.
What happens to historical data?
Classify data by operational, reporting, legal and audit need. Some data may be migrated, some retained in a governed archive and some retired according to policy.
How do we reduce cutover risk?
Use early validation, reconciled data, role-based testing, clear acceptance criteria, contingency planning and a transition schedule aligned to business cycles.
