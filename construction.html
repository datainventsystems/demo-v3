FAQ

Project, integration and practical concerns

Straight answers to common questions construction buyers consider when connecting field, project and business systems.

Can DataInvent Systems connect Microsoft solutions with existing systems?
Integration and compatibility are explicit requirements in DataInvent Systems's buyer journey for technical evaluators, and the site architecture includes Microsoft solution and technology paths for that validation.
How does the construction experience connect field and office teams?
The construction journey is designed around project efficiency and jobsite operations, with relevant solution paths for collaboration, workflows, finance and analytics.
Can Construction Cloud fit into the industry journey?
Yes. The approved information architecture calls for Construction Cloud to become proof of vertical depth inside the Construction industry experience rather than remain isolated from the industry journey.
Will field teams actually use it?
Workflows have to be practical for the people capturing and using information on site. We design around how field teams work, keep the steps simple, and train the people who will use them, so the information reaching the office is current.
How do you change systems without disrupting active projects?
Modernization is sequenced around active teams and projects, so change stays manageable. We agree what moves first, what stays in place for now, and when each team switches over.
