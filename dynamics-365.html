FAQ

Before choosing Dynamics 365.

Practical answers for teams evaluating Dynamics 365 and planning the next step.

Is Dynamics 365 one product?
No. Dynamics 365 is Microsoft’s family of business applications, with two sides: CRM for customer-facing work, and ERP for finance and operations. You choose the applications each side needs.
What’s included in Dynamics 365 CRM?
The customer-facing applications: Dynamics 365 Sales, Customer Service, Field Service, Customer Insights and Project Operations. You may also hear this side called Customer Engagement (CE).
What’s included in Dynamics 365 ERP?
The finance and operations applications: Business Central for small and mid-sized organizations, and Finance and Supply Chain Management for larger, more complex operations. The enterprise applications are often called Finance & Operations (F&O).
Where does Business Central fit?
Business Central is part of Dynamics 365. It’s Microsoft’s all-in-one ERP for small and mid-sized organizations, and DataInvent Systems covers it in depth on its own technology page.
Do we need all of these applications?
No. The scope should follow the business processes and outcomes that need to change, not the size of the Dynamics 365 catalogue.
