HIRING PROCESS

A clear path from application to onboarding.

Five focused stages help candidates understand what comes next throughout the DataInvent hiring journey.

01

Take the first step

Submit your application through our careers page. Our recruitment team will review your profile and reach out if your experience aligns with the role.

02

HR Screening

Have a conversation with our HR team to discuss your experience, skills, career goals, and expectations, and learn more about the role and DataInvent.

03

Technical Evaluation

Depending on the role, you'll go through a technical assessment or interview designed to evaluate your technical expertise, problem-solving skills, and practical knowledge.

04

Leadership Evaluation

Meet with DataInvent leadership for a focused conversation about role alignment, contribution, working approach, and the opportunity ahead.

05

Offer & Onboarding

If selected, we'll extend an offer and guide you through the onboarding process so you can start your journey with DataInvent smoothly.
