HIRING PROCESS

A clear path from application to onboarding.

We keep the process focused and transparent, so candidates understand what comes next and what each stage is designed to assess.

01

Apply

Submit your application through the relevant role page. The recruitment team reviews your experience against the role requirements.

02

HR Conversation

A short conversation about your background, interests, availability and what you are looking for next.

03

Technical or Role Evaluation

Depending on the position, complete an interview, practical task or discussion related to the work.

04

Leadership Conversation

Meet with a DataInvent leader to discuss expectations, ways of working and your potential contribution.

05

Offer and Onboarding

If selected, receive the offer and pre-boarding guidance needed for a confident start.

CAREER FAQ

Questions before
you apply?

Find clear answers about applications, interviews, internships and working at DataInvent.

Do you offer remote or hybrid roles?
Work arrangements depend on the role, team, customer requirements and location. The role description will state the approved arrangement.
How does the interview process work?
Most candidates complete an HR conversation, a role-specific assessment or interview, and a leadership discussion. The recruiter will explain the exact stages.
Does DataInvent offer internships?
Internship availability varies throughout the year. Current programmes and eligibility requirements will be listed in the early-career section.
Do I need Microsoft certifications?
Not for every role. Certifications may be required or preferred for some technical positions, while practical experience and willingness to learn remain important.
Can I apply if my role is not listed?
Yes. Submit a general application to join the talent network for future opportunities aligned with your experience.
