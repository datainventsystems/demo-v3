The customer

A trusted financial partner for its members.

The Power Sector Retirement Trust (PSRT) has been dedicated to serving its members for more than 20 years by providing a robust investment plan designed to achieve above-average returns while keeping management fees low.

By focusing on delivering superior returns and maintaining cost-efficiency, PSRT ensures that its members’ investments grow more effectively. This dedication reflects its commitment to enhancing the financial security and prosperity of its members over the long term.

The solution

One unified CRM on Dynamics 365 and the Power Platform.

The PSRT CRM, built on the Dynamics 365 Power Platform, consolidates PSRT’s operations into a unified, efficient system. It handles employer remittances, insurance coverage, and retirement funds.

Notable features include benefit trust management and retirement tracking, enabling users to simulate coverage, issue low fund letters, calculate plans, generate reports, and track dues. The system also manages apprenticeships and member skills, and integrates smoothly with member portals, banking systems, and an employer interface.
