FAQ

CRM & Sales questions

Straight answers to common questions before a first conversation.

Is this only for sales teams?
No. The same Microsoft platform can support customer, member, partner, service or case lifecycles.
Can CRM connect to finance or operations?
Yes, where the data ownership and business event are defined. Integration scope should identify the system of record, direction, timing, errors and support model.
How do we improve user adoption?
Reduce unnecessary fields, design by role, involve users in validation, provide workflow guidance and measure whether the system supports real work.
Can we migrate our existing data?
Yes, after profiling, deduplication, mapping and decisions about history. Data quality and ownership must be addressed before migration effort is committed.
Can the solution support a portal?
Potentially. Audience, authentication, data exposure, workflow, accessibility and support requirements must be confirmed.
