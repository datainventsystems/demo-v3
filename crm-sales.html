FAQ

CRM & Sales questions

Straight answers to common questions before a first conversation.

Can Dynamics 365 Sales work with our existing Microsoft tools?
The solution can be designed around Microsoft 365, Power Platform, Power BI, Azure and the systems that already support the revenue process.
Do we need to replace every customer system at once?
No. Discovery identifies the priority source systems, integrations and workflows so the CRM journey can be phased around business value and continuity.
How do you improve CRM adoption?
Adoption is addressed through role-based design, reduced administrative friction, practical training and clear ownership for data and process governance.
Can sales automation be tailored to our process?
Yes. Dynamics 365 configuration and Power Platform extensions can support the stages, approvals, routing and handoffs required by the operating model.
What happens after launch?
Post-launch support focuses on usage, data quality, workflow refinement and reporting so the platform continues to improve with the sales organization.
