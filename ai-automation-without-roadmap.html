Objection Handling

Questions leaders ask before scaling AI

Practical answers before deciding where to pilot, how to govern and what it will take to scale responsibly.

Do we need a complete enterprise AI strategy before starting?
No. A focused roadmap can begin with a small number of valuable and feasible use cases while establishing the minimum governance, data and adoption foundations needed to learn responsibly.
How do we avoid pilots that never move into daily operations?
Define the operational owner, workflow change, success measures, data requirements and support model before the pilot begins. A pilot should test the path to adoption, not only the technology.
How do we choose which AI use cases to prioritize?
Compare use cases using business value, feasibility, data readiness, risk and adoption effort. The strongest starting point is usually a visible problem with an accountable owner and a practical way to measure improvement.
How should governance fit into an AI roadmap?
Governance should shape use-case selection and design from the beginning. Practical governance clarifies acceptable use, data access, human review, accountability and escalation without becoming a late-stage blocker.
What if employees are already using AI tools informally?
Treat existing usage as an adoption and governance signal. Understand where value is appearing, identify the risks and provide approved patterns, training and support rather than relying only on prohibition.
