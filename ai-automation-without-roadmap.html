Objection Handling

Questions leaders ask before scaling AI

Practical answers before deciding where to pilot, how to govern and what it will take to scale responsibly.

Should we start with Copilot or a custom agent?
Start with the work and outcome, not the tool. Existing Copilot capabilities may fit common productivity needs, while an agent or custom solution may be appropriate when the process, data and actions are more specific.
Do we need perfect data first?
No, but the data and knowledge used by the selected use case must be sufficiently accurate, accessible and governed. Readiness should be evaluated use case by use case.
How do we choose the first use case?
Prioritize a repeatable task with clear users, accessible information, manageable risk, measurable effort or quality impact and an accountable process owner.
Can AI automate approvals and decisions?
Some steps can be assisted, but policy, risk and accountability determine where human review remains necessary. Deterministic rules may be better handled by workflow automation.
How do we measure value?
Define a baseline and measure the specific outcome: time saved, cycle time, rework, quality, adoption, response time or another approved indicator. Avoid broad productivity claims without evidence.
How do we manage security?
Apply least privilege, information governance, approved data boundaries, logging, human oversight and a clear acceptable-use model appropriate to the use case.
