Operating context

What shapes modernization in the public sector.

Public-sector teams balance service delivery, oversight, security and compliance while working across programs, stakeholders and legacy systems. Modernization has to improve the experience without weakening governance or operational continuity.

WHAT SHAPES THE WORK
  • Case, request and stakeholder workflows
  • Compliance, auditability and policy controls
  • Identity, permissions and data protection
  • Reporting, analytics and cross-team visibility
