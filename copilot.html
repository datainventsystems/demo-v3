FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Practical answers for teams evaluating Microsoft Copilot and planning the next step.

What is Microsoft Copilot?
Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's family of AI assistants. It helps with writing, summarizing, analysis and search in Microsoft 365 apps, business applications and Copilot Chat. Features and licensing differ by product and plan, so DataInvent confirms what fits your environment.
Is our data safe with Copilot?
Copilot only uses data that each person is already permitted to access, and Microsoft states that prompts and responses are not used to train its foundation models. Because results reflect your permissions, a readiness review of access and sensitivity labels is part of a good rollout.
Do we need Copilot Studio?
Not to start. Many teams begin with Copilot in their everyday apps and add agents later for specific processes. DataInvent helps you decide where an agent is worth building.
How do we get people to use it?
Adoption is planned from the start: a pilot group, role-based scenarios, training, and usage reporting that shows where more support is needed.
Where should we start with Copilot?
Start where people repeat the same work every week, such as meeting follow-up, email, reporting or document drafting. We choose a small pilot group with clear success measures, then widen the rollout based on what the pilot shows.
