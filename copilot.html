FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Practical answers for teams evaluating Microsoft Copilot and planning the next step.

What’s the difference between Copilot Chat and Microsoft 365 Copilot?
Copilot Chat is a secure AI chat available to Microsoft 365 users. A Microsoft 365 Copilot licence adds Copilot inside your apps, full access to your work data (emails, files, meetings, chats), agents and more advanced features. Small businesses can also get Copilot bundled with Microsoft 365 Business Standard or Business Premium.
Is our data safe with Copilot?
Yes. Copilot respects your existing Microsoft 365 permissions, sensitivity labels and compliance policies, and your data isn’t used to train the underlying models. The biggest risk is usually oversharing (files open to more people than intended), which is why we fix permissions before rollout.
Do we need to be ‘ready’ before turning Copilot on?
Usually some prep helps: the right licences, clean permissions, labels and a few priority use cases. Our Readiness Assessment tells you exactly what’s needed, so you don’t roll out into a data mess.
How long does implementation take?
It depends on your size and package. A small business can be up and running in a few weeks; larger rollouts run in phases. We’ll give you a clear timeline after the assessment.
Can Copilot work with our ERP or CRM?
Yes. Through connectors, plugins and Copilot Studio, Copilot can use data from Dynamics 365, Business Central and many third-party systems.
What are agents, and do we need them?
Agents are AI assistants focused on a specific job, like answering HR questions or processing requests. Many teams start with Copilot alone and add agents once they see where repeat work piles up.
How do we know it’s working?
We set up usage and adoption reporting (such as the Copilot Dashboard) and agree success measures up front, like hours saved or tasks automated.
Can you supply our Copilot licences?
Yes. As a Microsoft partner, we can provide and manage your licences and check that you have the prerequisites in place.
