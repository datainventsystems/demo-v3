FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Practical answers for teams evaluating Microsoft 365 and planning the next step.

What is included in Microsoft 365?
Depending on the plan, Microsoft 365 includes the Office apps, Teams, Exchange email, SharePoint and OneDrive, together with identity, security and device management. DataInvent Systems helps you choose the plan that fits.
Which Microsoft 365 plan do we need?
Business plans (Basic, Standard and Premium) suit organizations of up to 300 users, with Business Premium adding device management and advanced security. Enterprise plans add deeper security, compliance and scale. Plans can be mixed by role, and we recommend the right mix as part of planning.
Can you migrate us from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365?
Yes. We move Gmail, Google Calendar, contacts and Google Drive files to Outlook, OneDrive and SharePoint. People keep their existing email addresses, and the migration is planned to limit disruption.
Will we lose any emails when we switch to Microsoft 365?
Existing mail is copied before the switch, and new mail continues to arrive during the transition. Your domain is switched over only after the migration has been checked.
We already use Microsoft 365. Can you improve what we have?
Yes. We review your current setup and Secure Score, switch on appropriate protections already available in your licensing, and tidy Teams and SharePoint so content is easier to find and govern.
How do you keep Microsoft 365 secure?
By planning identity, devices and data together: multifactor sign-in, Conditional Access, device management, data protection labels and monitoring, set up to match your risks.
How does this prepare us for Copilot?
Copilot works with the content and permissions already in Microsoft 365. Well-organized files and tidy permissions make it safer and more useful, so readiness work is part of the plan.
