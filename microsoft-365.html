FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Practical answers for teams evaluating Microsoft 365 and planning the next step.

What is included in Microsoft 365?
Depending on the plan, Microsoft 365 includes the Office apps, Teams, Exchange email, SharePoint and OneDrive, together with identity, security and device management. DataInvent helps you choose the plan that fits.
Can you migrate us from another platform?
Yes. The scope for mail, files and identities is defined during discovery, with a plan that limits disruption for your teams.
How do you keep Microsoft 365 secure?
By planning identity, devices and data together: multifactor sign-in, Conditional Access, device management, data protection labels and monitoring, set up to match your risks.
How does this prepare us for Copilot?
Copilot works with the content and permissions already in Microsoft 365. Well-organized files and tidy permissions make it safer and more useful, so readiness work is part of the plan.
How do you help people adopt new ways of working?
Training, champions and clear communication help new ways of working stick. We also set up governance for Teams and SharePoint, so content stays organized and easy to find as usage grows.
