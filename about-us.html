Message from the Chairman

Turning complexity into decisive clarity.

If you're reading this, you're deciding whether to trust someone with something that matters. Fair. Somewhere in every growing business there's a spreadsheet nobody dares touch, a report that never matches the last one, and one person who holds it all in their head. That's where we're usually asked to start.

Microsoft gives our clients a strong, secure platform, and we're proud to be its partner. A platform won't fix a process on its own. So we listen first, work out what's really slowing you down, and build in stages so the business keeps running while it changes. Then we stay, because go-live is where the real work begins.

What I'm proudest of isn't on a certificate, though we have a few. It's the people. We're a team across five countries who look out for each other and take ownership of the work, and this year Great Place to Work® certified DataInvent Systems in Canada. I think you'll feel that when you work with us.

Our Journey

Built in Mississauga. Grown by our people.

It began as one office in 2012. Today we are a team across five countries. This is how it happened, one decision at a time.

201220162018202020242026
2012

The Beginning

One office. One idea.

We opened our first office in Mississauga, Ontario, as a technology consulting and business solutions company. The idea was simple: help organizations turn digital transformation from a plan into something that works.

2016

Microsoft Business Applications Partnership

Our Microsoft roots deepen.

We became a Microsoft Business Applications Partner and went deeper into Dynamics 365 and Power Platform, so customers could rely on us for more of what runs their business.

2018

Nationwide Expansion

From one city to coast to coast.

A Mississauga firm became a Canadian one. We took on clients from coast to coast and grew the team of specialists to look after them properly.

2020

Microsoft Solutions Excellence

All six Microsoft designations.

We earned all six Microsoft Solution Partner designations. Behind every one are certified people and real customer projects, across Microsoft's cloud and business technology.

2024

Expansion into the United States

Crossing the border.

We opened our doors in the U.S., bringing the same consulting, cloud and business application work our Canadian clients already knew to organizations across North America.

2026

Global Growth

A wider world, and a proud year.

New operations in the UAE, Morocco and Asia took us international. And in November we became Great Place to Work® Certified in Canada, which says more about our people than any market we enter.
