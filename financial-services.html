FAQ

Compliance, data and practical concerns

Straight answers to common questions financial-services buyers consider when modernizing regulated finance, data and technology environments.

How does security fit into the financial-services journey?
Security, governance and integration are explicit concerns for IT buyers, while financial leaders also need confidence around risk, control and compliance.
Can modernization connect to existing business systems?
The approved buyer journey treats compatibility and integration as core technical validation needs, so the modernization path should account for the existing stack rather than assume a disconnected replacement.
How should proof be presented to finance leaders?
The approved persona brief calls for ROI, cost, control, compliance and finance case-study proof to support CFO and finance-leader evaluation.
Can we use automation and AI in a regulated environment?
Yes, where each use case is assessed first. We look at data access, approvals and audit trail requirements, and build automation into controlled processes so efficiency does not come at the cost of oversight.
How do you keep finance and operations running during the change?
We agree the order of change and the cutover points in advance, so critical processes such as close and reporting are protected while modernization is under way.
